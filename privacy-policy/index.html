What data we collect

Font Explorer does not require accounts or collect personal identifiers. The only data stored is your on-device preferences: favorite fonts, recently viewed fonts, and your theme choice.

How we use data

The local preferences above are used to personalize your experience. They are saved in your browser storage and never transmitted to our servers.

Cookies and analytics

We do not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics by default. If that ever changes, this policy will be updated with clear notice.

Third-party services

Font previews link to Google Fonts. Visiting those links is optional and subject to Google's own policies.
